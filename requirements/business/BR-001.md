# BR-001: Subscription renewal reminder

## Description
The system shall notify users about upcoming subscription renewals to improve retention and user experience.

## Rationale
Proactive renewal reminders reduce churn by giving users advance notice to renew their subscriptions.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Users receive email notification 7 days before subscription expires
- Users receive email notification 1 day before subscription expires  
- Users receive follow-up email 3 days after expiration if not renewed

## Dependencies
None

## Notes
Email frequency and timing should be configurable for different subscription tiers.
