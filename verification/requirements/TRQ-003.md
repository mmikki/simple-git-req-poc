# TRQ-003: Verify post-expiry follow-up notifications

## Objective
Verify that follow-up notifications are sent to users after their subscriptions have expired.

## Test Scope
- Follow-up notification scheduling after expiry
- Follow-up message content (renewal incentives, special offers)
- User preference suppression of follow-up notifications
- Delivery status tracking
- Multiple follow-up message templates

## Success Criteria
- Follow-up notifications sent at configured days after expiry
- Messages include renewal incentives or special offers
- Users can suppress follow-up notifications
- Notification delivery is tracked and logged
- Different message templates can be used
