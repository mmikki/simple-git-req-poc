# TRQ-001: Verify email notification delivery

## Objective
Verify that email notifications are sent correctly to users at configured intervals before subscription expiry.

## Test Scope
- Email sending with correct recipient address
- Email content includes subscription details
- Emails sent at configured offset times (7 days, 1 day before expiry)
- Email delivery status tracking
- Retry mechanism for failed sends

## Success Criteria
- Emails are successfully sent to valid addresses
- Email content contains correct subscription information
- Notifications respect configured timing offsets
- Failed deliveries are logged and retried
- All sending events are auditable
